### [Measuring and Predicting Right-Wing Populism in Britain](https://github.com/nicksuciu7/right-wing-populism-britain)

My BSc dissertation brings together five British Social Attitudes survey waves to study how demographic and social characteristics relate to a survey-based attitude score.

- **Data engineering:** harmonised changing survey variables, response codes and missing data across 2011–2024.
- **Analysis and modelling:** built a PCA-informed score and evaluated survey-weighted ridge regression against simple baselines, including transfer to a held-out survey wave.
- **Reproducibility:** packaged the Python pipeline with pinned dependencies, automated tests, documented methodology and aggregate results that can be explored without licensed data.
- **Research communication:** published a reading edition of the dissertation, visual explanations, limitations and a transparent correction audit.

**Python · pandas · NumPy · scikit-learn · Matplotlib · Git**

[Explore the project](https://github.com/nicksuciu7/right-wing-populism-britain) · [Read the dissertation](https://github.com/nicksuciu7/right-wing-populism-britain/blob/main/dissertation/dissertation-public.pdf)
